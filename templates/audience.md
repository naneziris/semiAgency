# Audience brief
<!-- Fill this in before /write-storylines. 10 minutes. The storylines and the speech are only as good as this. -->

## Who is in the room
- roles, seniority, how many; who decides, who influences

## What they decide or do after this talk
- the one decision or action we need from them

## What they already know
- about the subject; about us; what they have been told before (and by whom)

## What they care about / are measured on
- cost, risk, time-to-market, compliance, headcount, customer impact … in their words

## What they will push back on
- the objections we expect, and who will raise them

## Language
- terms they use; jargon to avoid; technical concepts that MUST be explained anyway, and the analogy or business consequence to explain each with

## Tone
- e.g. direct, numbers-first, no hype; how long before they lose patience
