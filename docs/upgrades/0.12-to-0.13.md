# 0.12 → 0.13: the walkable city, scenarios and `new_prompt.py`

Scaffold files and shipped scripts only, no Copilot-built script changes. Easiest route: the manual upgrade in `README.md` here (new ZIP + your own folders); the text-only patch works too.

## What's new
- **`index.html` is a walkable city**: the painting, always fully in view, with a pixel hero who walks the streets (WASD or click; he finds his way around buildings and water). Same content as before — one area per flow, one building per step, the piers for the M365 scenarios, the Front Desk — but clicking a building or picking it in the panel walks the hero there and opens it. No libraries, offline, works on a phone.
- **Scenarios**: the "I want to…" list is now the scenarios list. Pick one and the panel shows its steps one line each, flagged *required* or *optional* (`"optional": true` on a stop in `city.json`), with a copy chip for every command and for every M365 prompt you paste elsewhere; the stops are numbered on the map and **Next step** walks you there.
- **Prompt library** tab in the panel: every prompt and agent with Copy, Download `.prompt.md` / `.agent.md`, and *Download all (.zip)* for colleagues who want them in their own workspace. Each building's detail also has a download button per prompt, agent and sheet.
- **`scripts/new_prompt.py`** (shipped): adds a prompt in one go — the file with a skeleton body, its building, its stop on a route (or a new route), then rebuilds the map and `docs/menu.md`. Before, a hand-added prompt landed in New Arrivals and failed `build_menu.py --check` until someone edited `city.json` twice.
- **`city/template.html`** holds the page (CSS, canvas world, panel); `build_city.py` only assembles. Edit the template to change the look. `--link-image` is gone; the painting is always embedded.

## On the corporate machine
Nothing to build. After the upgrade: `python scripts/build_city.py` and open `index.html`. `python scripts/selftest_all.py` now covers `new_prompt.py`.
