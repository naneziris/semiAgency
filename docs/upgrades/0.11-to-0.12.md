# 0.11 → 0.12: the `/start` guide

Scaffold files and shipped scripts only, no Copilot-built script changes. Easiest route: the manual upgrade in `README.md` here (new ZIP + your own folders); otherwise `git apply upgrade-0.11-to-0.12.patch` (text only, no `--binary` needed), then `python scripts/build_city.py`.

## What's new
- **`/start` and `@guide`**: one command to remember. The guide reads `engagements/CURRENT` and `state.json`, and either gives you the one next step or asks what you want and points you to the right flow. It is read-only: it never runs a stage or edits a file. It gets "where am I" from the new read-only `scripts/where.py` (shipped), so it works without `status.py`, and small local models get it right.
- **`/new-flow` and the wishlist**: when `/start` can't match a wish it records it in `wishlist.md` (gitignored, `scripts/wish.py`) and offers `/new-flow`, where `@builder` interviews you (five questions at most), shows a one-screen design, and after your yes writes the prompt, instructions and map entry and rebuilds the menu. The Architect's Office on the map.
- **`docs/menu.md`**: the routing table the guide reads. It is generated from `city/city.json` by `scripts/build_menu.py`, so the map and the guide cannot disagree. `--check` fails when a prompt can't be reached from the menu.
- **The Front Desk** on the map now claims `/start` and `@guide`. The Town Hall and the Cartographer mention them.

## On the corporate machine
Nothing to build. After the upgrade, type `/start` in Copilot Chat. New shipped scripts (`where.py`, `wish.py`, `build_menu.py`) come with the ZIP or the patch; `python scripts/selftest_all.py` covers them. `wishlist.md` is gitignored, so it survives future upgrades like your other own files.
